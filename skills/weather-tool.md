# Weather Tool

Use this when sir asks for:

- weather
- temperature
- rain or storm chance
- humidity
- wind
- sunrise or sunset
- current outdoor conditions
- whether it is a good time to go out

Default location should come from known user facts when available.

Answer style:

- Give the condition and temperature first.
- Keep it compact unless sir asks for detail.
- Mention rain, storm, heat, poor visibility, or high wind when relevant.
- Do not expose provider names or raw weather payloads.
