# Summarize

- For URLs, call `extract_url_content` or `fetch_url_text` before summarizing.
- For local files, call `document_extract_text` or `read_text_file` before summarizing.
- Keep raw HTML, schemas, and tool narration out of the answer.
- If the extracted content is huge, summarize the available bounded content and say when it was truncated.
