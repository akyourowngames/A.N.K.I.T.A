---

**A Dog’s Heart**  

With wagging tail and soulful eyes,  
A loyal friend beneath the skies.  
Through every storm, through joy, through strife,  
A dog remains a part of life.  

They greet the dawn with boundless cheer,  
A bark, a leap, a love sincere.  
No judgment dwells within their gaze,  
Just endless love, through all our days.  

Their paws may tread on paths unknown,  
Yet always find their way back home.  
A gentle nudge, a playful bark,  
They light the world when it feels dark.  

Oh, faithful friend, so pure, so true,  
The world is brighter because of you.  
In every wag, in every glance,  
You teach us love, you teach us dance.  

So here’s to dogs, our hearts’ delight,  
Our morning joy, our stars at night.  
Forever loyal, forever near,  
Our furry angels, ever dear.
